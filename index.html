whoami?

I am Bendik Arbogast, a software developer pursuing a master's in computer science at ETH Zürich. I am especially interested in GPU accelerated workloads, mostly graphics and AI, starting with my render engine FlexLight.

To fully understand the math and logic behind graphics and AI, I implement from papers in the field instead of using libraries or code references. FlexLight runs directly in the browser, where WebGPU, and WebGL2 before it, enable cross-platform support.

For my bachelor thesis at Disney Research I developed a PyTorch framework for gradient-based pose optimization from video through a frozen RenderFormer. In 2026 I interned in the graphics engine team of the Esri R&D Center Zurich, contributing to the ArcGIS Maps SDK and CityEngine.

Besides my studies I build web and automation solutions for clients at Arbogast Solutions.

FlexLight

Try FlexLight, a path tracer built with WebGPU

Written from scratch in TypeScript and manually optimized, with BVH acceleration, importance sampled BSDFs, physically based materials, dynamic scenes and NVIDIA's ReSTIR PT Enhanced. Source

FlexCudaPT

FlexCudaPT, a Blender render engine using OptiX or Vulkan

Path traces Blender scenes on RTX GPUs with FlexLight's BSDF and ReSTIR PT Enhanced, about 4x faster than Cycles and converging around 60x faster in equal time.
