whoami?

I am Bendik Arbogast, a software developer pursuing a master's degree in computer science at ETH Zürich.

Over the last years I developed a special interest in GPU accelerated workloads, mostly computer graphics and machine learning. It started with personal projects such as my render engine FlexLight, which is listed above.

As a principle, to fully understand the core concepts and the underlying math of technologies such as path tracing or ML, I abstain from using third party frameworks or libraries in those projects.

Most of them run directly in the browser. WebGPU, and WebGL2 before it, enable GPU accelerated workloads with ideal cross-platform support.

For my bachelor thesis at Disney Research I developed a PyTorch framework for gradient-based pose optimization through a frozen RenderFormer. In 2026 I worked as a software development intern in the graphics engine team of the Esri R&D Center Zurich.

Besides my studies I build web and automation solutions for clients as Arbogast Solutions.
