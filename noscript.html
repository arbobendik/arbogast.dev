whoami?


I am Bendik Arbogast a software developer
currently studying computer science at ETH Zürich.


In my spare time I enjoy programming and developing on my personal projects which are partially listed above.


As a principle to fully understand core concepts and underlying math of modern technology such as ML or Raytracing and to receive the maximal learning experience I abstain from using third party frameworks or libraries in those projects.


Over the last years I developed a special interest in GPU accelerated workloads and started projects - such as own render-engine FlexLight - or my neural-net library NeuNet
using glsl shaders.


One of my favourite technologies that I also used to implement those projects is WebGL2 - even if it is an old standard that hopefully will be replaced soon - since it enables GPU accelerated workloads directly in the browser with ideal cross-platform support.
